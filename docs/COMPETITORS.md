# Desh Chalao: competitors

**When:** written 2026-11-24. Data was seen on 2026-11-23 unless a claim says otherwise; a few items come from the 2026-11-22 idea sort.

**Product:** [IDEA.md](IDEA.md). This research does not use [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md); it is market research only.

**How it was done:**
- Seven researchers ran in parallel: Lapse, Reigns, MA 2, Hidden Lake, the India-set games, the graveyard, and demand signals.
- They hand-read **1,538 three-star reviews** (plus 618 two-star reviews) from Play's own review endpoint.
- I ran Google Trends myself.
- Sections 4, 7 and 8 are my judgement, built on their evidence.

**Labels**, beside every claim with its link and date:
- **SEEN**: opened, or fetched first-hand.
- **REPORTED**: someone else says so.
- **ESTIMATED**: a tool's guess, or my own arithmetic.

Link keys (e.g. [IEG]) are defined at the end and are clickable. Review quotes are verbatim, with the review's own date and stars. Reviewer names are never stored.

---

## 0. The answer in five lines

1. **There is a market.** Political and ruler sims are played every day and paid for:
   - MA 2 – President Simulator has 1 crore+ installs and gets about 2.5 English reviews a day in 2026.
   - Reigns sells for ₹250 upfront and has an estimated $6.2m in lifetime revenue.
2. **Games set in India are thinly served, small and growing.**
   - The most direct competitor, Indian Election Game, reached 50K+ installs in about 8 months, then stalled.
   - Search interest in India for "election game" has risen every year since 2021.
3. **The gap is real but narrow.** Players of Indian Election Game ask, in their own words, for exactly Desh Chalao's core:
   - 48 of its 418 reviews ask to govern after winning;
   - 10 ask for a career ladder, like "party worker to MLA, MP, CM, PM".
   No one offers that in India's own system today. A generic version ("party activist… to the president of a country") launched on 12 Nov 2026 with 100+ installs (§1.6).
4. **Four things are against it:**
   - the evidence of the gap sits mostly in one small app's reviews;
   - a generic career-ladder sim with the same "no timers, no money walls" promise appeared on 12 Nov 2026 (Politician Career Simulator, §1.6);
   - a new entrant, The Mandate (Sept 2026, fictional parties, "start as a political nobody"), is heading for the same spot;
   - the #1 complaint across the whole genre, running out of content, will be just as hard for you.
5. **Lead with this:** a proven genre, a thinly served Indian niche, and a real but narrow wedge. Treat it as a niche bet with a clear angle, not a mass market.

## 7. The parity trap

### What users expect on day one because every competitor has it

**Build these. You can't refuse them:**
1. **Free to install.** Everyone is free except Reigns, and its paid model draws 39 "not worth the price" complaints.
2. **Fast tap or swipe choices with visible meters and instant feedback.**
3. **Save and resume, never losing progress.** Complaint #3.
4. **Pick your state.**
   - Indian Election Game has all states, MA 2 all countries, GovSim 26 nations.
   - Your spec already has this (**FACT**), as a list, with no map.
5. **A guide in the first minute.** Complaint #6.
6. **Basic sound and music.** Hidden Lake President's 3★ reviews include "There is no background music" (6 people).
7. **Works on tall phones and foldables.** Complaint #9.
8. **Endings, and a way to play again.**
9. **Hindi.**
   - Indian Election Game ships English and Hindi.
   - Players ask Hidden Lake and MA 2 for it.
   - For a game set in India it's expected, and it's your **FACT** anyway.
10. **Offline.**
    - Indian Election Game sells "Fully offline".
    - Hidden Lake's need for internet draws complaints.

### Refuse these, and still survive

- **Real parties and leaders** (Indian Election Game): refuse.
  - The legal reasons are in RESEARCH.
  - Indian Election Game shows the cost: 14 bias claims.
  - The Mandate shows a fictional game can launch (5K+, 4.38★), though that is thin evidence.
- **A map of India** (MA 2, Indian Election Game, Elections of India): refuse. MA 2's 1★ reviews fight over its map.
- **Multiplayer:** refuse in v1, although it is the most-asked "extra". Offline is a **FACT**.
- **A detailed seat-by-seat election simulation:** refuse in v1. The evidence says Indian Election Game's players want governance after the vote. Keep elections as a results screen.
- **Many countries:** refuse. India only.
- **Ads, timers, energy and paywalls mid-story:** refuse. That is complaint #5, and RESEARCH's DPDP problem.
- **Paid hints that reveal a choice's effects** (Lapse's Oracle): refuse. It sells the clarity people complain about.
- **Cloud save, achievements, leaderboards:** later.

## 8. The honest verdict

### Is there a market? **Yes for the genre. Players are proven for the Indian niche; money is not yet.**

- **Played every week, at scale:**

  - Hidden Lake President: 1M+ installs in about 15 months.

  - SEEN

  - in-app purchases up to ₹11,300 per item.
  - SEEN
- **India-set political games are played weekly:**
  - Indian Election Game: 50K+ installs in about 8 months, all 418 reviews from 2026.
  - Elections of India: 50K+.
  - Search interest for "election game" in India has risen from a weekly median of 1 to 10 since 2021.
  - SEEN / ESTIMATED
- **Nobody yet visibly pays for an India-set one.** Indian Election Game removed purchases; Elections of India's revenue is unknown.

### Is there a gap? **Yes, real but narrow. The evidence is specific, but most of it comes from one small app.**

1. **Govern after winning, plus a career ladder.**
   - 48 of 418 Indian Election Game reviews (11%) ask to govern after winning, and 10 ask for "party worker to MLA, MP, CM, PM". MA 2 players ask for parliament, opposition, and states with chief ministers.
   - Nobody offers India's MLA → CM → PM career with governing at each rung:
     - Politician Career Simulator: a generic activist-to-president ladder with governing, launched 12 Nov 2026; India not verified;
     - Indian Election Game: elections only, and stalled;
     - The Mandate: campaigns only, by its own description, and new;
     - Hidden Lake: starts you at the top and resets you;
     - MA 2: a war sandbox.
2. **Monetisation without friction.** Paywalls, ads and timers are deliberate at Hidden Lake, MA 2 and Lapse, and they're complaint #5.
3. **Clarity.** The swipe games hide what a choice does on purpose.
4. **Hindi that works.**

**Read the caveats just as plainly:**
- (a) The core evidence of the gap is about 58 reviews in one small app.
- (b) **The Mandate is moving toward the same spot**, and it already uses fictional parties.
- (c) In 2026 the attention goes to satire about real politicians, which Desh Chalao won't do.
- (d) The leading India-set app peaked at 50K+ installs.
- (e) **The genre's #1 complaint, running out of content, is just as hard for you.**
- (f) A generic career-ladder sim (Politician Career Simulator, 12 Nov 2026) already promises "no timers, no money walls".

**Lead:** *Well served globally, with a thin but genuine angle in India.* Players of the only direct competitor are asking for Desh Chalao's exact core, and that competitor has stopped updating. That is a real opening. It is a niche, and it will be won by content depth and steady updates, not by the idea alone.

| A seat-by-seat election simulation | Indian Election Game | That is their game. Our players asked for what comes after the vote. Elections stay a result screen that judges your record. |
| War, conquest, other countries | MA 2 | Focus on India's system. Complaint #3's war mechanics and AI "rules" stay their problem. |
| Multiplayer or online play | Elections of India, MA 2 chat | Offline is the promise: it works anywhere, with no tracking. Multiplayer needs servers and moderation (RESEARCH). |
| Timers, energy, ads on death, mid-story paywalls, paid hints | Hidden Lake, MA 2, Lapse | These are complaints #5 (115+ people) and #7 (71). Refusing them is the "better" a player feels in minute one. |
| Loot boxes, real money | — | The online gaming law, and trust (RESEARCH). |
| Satire or mockery of real politicians | the viral 2026 videos (§1.7) | That is where the attention is, and we leave it. Our pitch is "learn how it works by doing it". |
| News or real-election content | — | The sensitive-events rule and neutrality (RESEARCH). Timing the marketing is enough. |
| Accounts and sign-up | — | Nothing to protect and nothing to delete (RESEARCH). |
| Unsourced or AI-written "facts" | Hidden Lake uses AI for stories ("While AI helps generate dynamic stories…", developer reply, 2025-10-09 · SEEN), and 2 people at 3★ complain about AI art | Every explainer carries a public source (**FACT**). That is the trust a civics game needs. |

> **For Indian players who love politics but only get to watch it, Desh Chalao is the Hindi-and-English career game that lets you govern your own state as an MLA and earn your way to CM and then PM, unlike election games that stop at the vote count and global president sims that pause your term for a paywall.**
